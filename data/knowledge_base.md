# Harbour Dental Clinic: front desk knowledge base

(A fictional clinic used for this demo. Swap this file for a real client's FAQ.)

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.
- Saturday, 10am to 2pm.
- Closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Address and parking
- 14 Harbour Street, Unit 3, ground floor, next to the pharmacy.
- Free parking for patients behind the building. Disabled parking spaces by the entrance.
- The entrance has step-free access.

## Services and prices
- New patient check-up with X-rays: 60 pounds, 30 minutes.
- Routine check-up for existing patients: 40 pounds, 30 minutes.
- Hygienist clean: 55 pounds, 30 minutes.
- Teeth whitening consultation: free, 30 minutes.
- Treatment prices beyond these are given by the dentist after a check-up, never quoted on the phone.

## Payment and insurance
- Card, Apple Pay and cash accepted. Payment is taken on the day.
- The clinic accepts most major dental insurance plans; patients should bring their policy number.

## Appointments
- Every appointment slot is 30 minutes.
- New patients should arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a medical history form.
- Cancellations need 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and missed appointments are charged 20 pounds.

## Emergencies
- Same-day emergency slots are held each morning for patients in pain. Callers in pain should be handed to a member of the team.
- For heavy bleeding, facial swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or a serious injury, the caller must hang up and call the emergency services straight away.
